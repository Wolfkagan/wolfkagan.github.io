# ÖTÜKEN AI Public Content Policy

This policy governs content intended for the public website. It is a repository-only review aid and must not be linked from public pages or copied into the production artifact.

## PUBLIC

Content that may be published:

- The approved brand name and slogans
- High-level purpose and positioning
- General research areas
- The four source principles
- Public responsibility layers
- Human-oversight emphasis
- Active-development status
- Verified contact information

## REVIEW REQUIRED

Content that requires explicit human review before publication:

- Benchmark results
- Technical papers
- Architecture diagrams
- Partner or institution names
- Team members
- Demonstration videos
- Code examples
- Roadmap dates
- Performance comparisons
- Data-source descriptions

## PRIVATE

Content that must not be published in the website artifact:

- Source code
- Internal architecture or security implementation
- Data schemas or system topology
- Hashes and internal manifests
- Local paths and hardware details
- Training recipes and model weights
- Internal prompts or credentials
- Evidence packages and operational logs
- Internal test output

Every public change must pass the translation-parity and public-disclosure audits before deployment.
