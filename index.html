01 / Platform thesis

Agents should be capable.
And answerable.

Most agent systems optimize for completion. ÖtükenAI is designed around a harder standard: preserving the connection between intent, evidence, authority, execution and review.

03 / Operating principles

Trust is engineered.

Our working principles shape both the product and the research behind it.

01

Evidence before assertion

Separate what is known, inferred and proposed. Preserve the path back to source.

02

Authority before action

Make permission boundaries explicit. Escalate when the requested authority expands.

03

Diagnosis before correction

Understand the failure mechanism before changing the system around it.

04

Verification before closure

Do not confuse a plausible answer with a completed and tested outcome.

05 / Company

Independent by design.

ÖtükenAI is an independent artificial intelligence initiative founded in Türkiye. We research and develop agent systems for complex environments where trust must be demonstrated through architecture—not promised through language.

Our work sits at the intersection of AI agents, human oversight, evidence systems and strategic decision support.
